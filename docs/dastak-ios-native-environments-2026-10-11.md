# Native Reimagined environments — initial implementation

Owner requested immediate native environment implementation on 11 October 2026. Canonical worktree/branch/HEAD unchanged; prior edits preserved. Customer only, solo execution; no image generation, paid asset purchases, backend changes or production deployment.

## Implemented locally

- Native SceneKit renderer behind SwiftUI, not a screenshot or WKWebView. Physically based materials, local scanned wood/pavement, refrigerator glazing, shelf/package geometry, ceiling rails, counter equipment and lights. Package shapes are decorative; they never provide SKU, price or availability data.
- Grocery entrance with two close shelf aisles, left refrigerators, right-wall stock geometry and a smaller left-facing checkout ahead of the right shelf. Explicit Take to Cart changes the camera to a frontal cashier view; closing cart returns to browsing. The underlying shopping panel hides while cart is open; the cart uses a glass sheet with half/full-height detents.
- Fast-service café with dining seating on both sides, a counter/menu-board wall and staff toward its right. Food basket content moves the camera toward the counter; empty Food returns to entrance. Decorative photographs have existing recorded rights and do not represent actual dishes.
- Outside storefront with no neighbouring buildings, tree planting on left/right, pavement/entry canopy/lights and day/night lighting. Signed-out Customer gets this outside background through an optional shared auth presentation hook. Default other-app auth behavior remains unchanged; no session/provider/access logic was altered.
- Existing CC0 MakeHuman character converted offline into six native textured meshes. The exporter poses arms/hands downward, constrains palm orientation, preserves actual eyelid deformation in face/brows/lashes, and retains shirt normal mapping. Native animation includes gentle whole-character breathing and a real eyelid morph blink; café adds an apron/cap. No runtime desk-target IK and no historical RPM character asset.
- Reduce Motion pauses nonessential movement and uses immediate camera changes. Inactive app stops rendering; dismantling detaches the scene. Target frame rates are 30 normally, 20 in Low Power, 15 under serious/critical thermal state. These are configured budgets, not measured performance claims. SceneKit is deprecated in newer SDKs; the renderer adapter is isolated for a future RealityKit migration.
- Day/night defaults to a labelled device-clock approximation. Settings provides explicit nearby-weather opt-in; no location request until Connect/consent, no stored location or reuse of delivery addresses, two-decimal coordinates, ephemeral credential/cookie-free weather requests, 15-minute throttling and one-hour expiry. Rain/snow particles and tree sway react to the weather input. The public Open-Meteo endpoint is Debug-only; Release requires a configured HTTPS service with suitable commercial rights via `DASTAK_SCENE_WEATHER_URL`. No service purchase/key/provisioning was performed, and no weather request was made in this verification pass.
- Isolated Debug scenery review (`-DastakEnvironmentPreview`) exposes Outside/Grocery entrance/billing/Café entrance/counter and Day/Night/Rain/Snow presets, explicitly labelled simulated. Optional launch arguments `-DastakEnvironmentPlace` and `-DastakEnvironmentWeather` select them without commerce transport. Normal Customer shopping uses real model state, not preview controls.
