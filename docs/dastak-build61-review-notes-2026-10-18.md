## App Review Notes

REVIEW ACCESS — BUILD 61
Dastak combines Customer grocery/food ordering and an approved Delivery partner workspace. Review both without an account: on the sign-in screen, tap "Explore the demo". The public demo uses the actual Customer and Delivery screens with isolated fictional data; it is clearly marked DEMO. Normal Apple/Google sign-in remains available. No shared credentials or production Delivery approval are needed for the demo.

WALKTHROUGH
1. The Demo guide opens on entry; tap Explore to begin. In Customer, choose Grocery or Food, pick up the basket before adding items, then browse products/menus, change quantities or pack sizes, select a sample address and place a sample order. Confirm the doorstep-payment option. The order first enters "Preparing your order".
2. Tap Guide, then "Sample merchant: mark order ready". This explicitly simulates merchant preparation and advances to "Assigning your rider".
3. Use the top Customer/Delivery selector to switch to Delivery. Refresh and accept the sample offer. Follow the pickup-arrival, collection and route steps, then mark arrival at the customer. Both pickup and customer PINs are 123456. Package count is 1. Use the sample photo action, record a simulated cash collection and complete the delivery.
4. View full order details and completed work/history in Delivery. Switch back to Customer to see tracking and completion. Repeat with the other service after Reset if desired.
5. To exercise returns, after delivery (or a reported delivery problem after pickup), tap Guide > "Sample Operations: assign a return". Follow the return steps using the sample photo and PIN 123456 for customer pickup and merchant receipt.

RESET / EXIT
Reset > Reset demo clears only the current sample session. Exit returns to normal sign-in. Real account data is not loaded or changed. Demo state remains during ordinary backgrounding but is not restored after terminating the app.

DEMO BOUNDARIES
No real purchase, dispatch, payment, refund, payout, identity/evidence upload or account-role change occurs. Sample arrival checks do not use GPS; calls and external links are disabled inside the demo. Account deletion, OAuth linking, real payouts and remote server behavior are not presented as simulated successes.

Optional Guide > "Enable local sample updates" demonstrates local notifications and Live Activity presentation on supported devices, subject to permissions/settings. It does not test remote APNs. Reset/Exit removes only this demo's sample system updates. Widgets, Shortcuts and controls remain navigation entry points.

LIVE SERVICE
Outside the demo, Customer uses Apple/Google sign-in and Delivery access requires approval. Real grocery/food fulfilment is limited to supported service locations; worldwide app availability does not imply worldwide delivery. The current checkout uses UPI/cash payment to the delivery partner at the doorstep, not an in-app online payment.
